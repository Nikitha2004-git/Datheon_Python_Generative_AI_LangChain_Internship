Sunrise Tech - Employee Leave Policy (Sample, fictional)

1. Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave must be requested at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year.

2. Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required if the absence lasts more than 3 consecutive days.

3. Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave.

4. Public holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays each year. The full list is published in January.

5. Work from home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
